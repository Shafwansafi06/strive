# Three-minute architecture demo

1. **Run Mid-call change.** The replay compresses 40 seconds of audio into a few seconds. At 5 seconds, see the initial profile become trusted. This means accepted by the test gate, not verified real-world identity.
2. **Inspect the change at 18 seconds.** Track 1 rises when a different procedural signal family appears. Track 2 measures inconsistency. The SPS stops accepting the changed windows. Follow the smoothed score and note the warning/alert delay; it is a result to assess, not hidden.
3. **Set the transfer to ₹40 lakh.** Toggle urgency or a new beneficiary and click Apply context. Acoustic risk stays unchanged; the decision risk and action may change.
4. **Attempt the mock transfer.** It is held. Choose a callback/MFA/supervisor method, confirm it was completed, record mock verification, then retry. No real transfer or verification service is contacted.
5. **Run Suspicious from the start.** The SPS stays empty throughout. Inspect active weights after 15 seconds; missing session evidence is masked. Notice the limits of the remaining fusion weights.
6. **Run Silence.** The score remains unavailable and the UI says Analyzing. Silence is never labelled genuine.
7. **Open the local app for real input.** Use microphone or upload, while explaining that the default DSP mode is an architecture surrogate. Meaningful clone detection experiments require the research model setup and actual speech data.

The portable HTML displays recorded backend results. For a fresh reproducible run, launch the server or run `python scripts/build_demo.py`. Do not present procedural test audio as a human/clone demonstration.
